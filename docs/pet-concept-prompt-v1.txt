Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: desktop pet character and key-pose concept sheet, version 1.

Primary request:
Design one original pixel-art desktop companion named Qiaoqiao for a local keyboard-counting application.
Show the same character in eight key poses on a clean landscape concept sheet.
This is a visual design proposal, not a finished animation spritesheet.

Reference:
assets/qiaoqiao-pixel.png is a style and product reference, not an edit target.
It depicts a compact mechanical-keycap motif with stepped pixel outlines, dark sidewalls,
light key surface, mint and pink accents.
Develop that keycap motif into a living character; preserve the existing application icon as-is.
The written description is sufficient if reference-image input is unavailable.

Character:
A friendly anthropomorphic mechanical keycap with a slightly wider-than-tall body.
The body itself is the head, with a flat light top facet, stepped corner cuts, and shaded sidewalls.
A small dark inset face panel holds two expressive mint pixel eyes.
Very short arms with simple mitten hands and short feet.
Small restrained pink cheek accents.
Quiet, curious, slightly shy personality.
No separate humanoid head, animal ears, tail, accessories, lettering or number display.

Style:
Deliberate low-resolution pixel art with crisp square pixels and a consistent pixel grid.
Design each pose as if on a 64 by 64 logical canvas, shown enlarged with nearest-neighbor scaling.
Approximately 34 by 30 logical pixels for the body and 46 by 44 including limbs in the neutral pose.
Two or three hard-edged tones per material, strong silhouette, no antialiasing or gradients.
Do not simulate pixel art using many tiny irregular pixels or painted texture.

Palette:
Outline #0D0C14.
Keycap front #F2EBF4.
Side planes #A49AB4 and #54465F.
Face panel #24202F.
Eyes and small limb accents #97E4C2.
Small pink accents #E59AAC.
Plain neutral light gray presentation background.

Composition:
Landscape sheet, exactly eight evenly spaced positions in two rows of four.
Same character scale and identity throughout, generous breathing room, no enclosing cards.
The bottom row uses fine charcoal boundary lines to explain screen-edge contact.
Boundary lines are presentation guides, not part of the character.
No text, labels, arrows, watermark, ornamental scenery or extra characters.

Top row, left to right:
1. Neutral free-standing idle pose, front view with a small visible top facet, hands relaxed, both feet planted.
2. The same idle pose at the high point of a very subtle breath, body raised one logical pixel while feet stay planted.
3. The same idle pose with eyes closed in a gentle blink; same silhouette and scale.
4. Picked-up drag pose, whole body visible, feet dangling naturally, hands slightly spread, eyes mildly widened.

Bottom row, left to right:
5. Peeking from the LEFT screen edge. A vertical line at the contact edge.
   Only about half of the body extends into the visible area to the RIGHT of that line.
   The visible face looks RIGHT toward screen interior; one hand visibly grips the edge.
   Draw a deliberate turned peeking pose, not a neutral frontal sprite sliced in half.
   Keep the visible eye and gripping hand intact.
6. Peeking from the RIGHT screen edge. A vertical line at the contact edge.
   Only about half of the body extends into the visible area to the LEFT of that line.
   The visible face looks LEFT toward screen interior; one hand visibly grips the edge.
   Match the identity, palette and proportions of pose 5.
7. Resting prone at the BOTTOM boundary. A horizontal line directly below the character.
   Body slightly lowered, chin close to both hands, hands and underside resting on the line,
   short feet tucked behind the body. All character pixels remain ABOVE the line.
   The line represents the top of the taskbar; no limbs hang into the taskbar.
8. Hanging from the TOP boundary. A horizontal line directly above the character.
   Both hands grip that line, body and short feet hang BELOW it.
   Contact points feel physically clear, face remains fully visible, small relaxed dangling pose.

Constraints:
Consistent body design, face panel, limb lengths, palette and pixel density in all eight poses.
Prioritize readable small-size silhouette, quiet expression and believable boundary contact.
No glossy 3D rendering, realistic metal, plush fur, soft shadows, glow or blurred outlines.
No keyboard props, taskbar app icons, full desktop screenshot or unrelated interface.
No high-amplitude jumping or excessive facial expression.
No claim that the sheet is a transparent or production-ready sprite asset.
