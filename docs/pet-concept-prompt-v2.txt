Keep:
One quiet, curious anthropomorphic keycap with a stepped silhouette,
an inset face panel, short arms and short feet.
No separate humanoid head, ears, tail, lettering or counter.
64 x 64 logical pixel canvas, nearest-neighbor enlargement.
Crisp square pixels, hard-edged shading, no glow, blur or smooth gradients.

Revise the palette throughout all poses:
Outline #0D0C14.
Body #443A53, a dark muted gray-purple.
Top facet #655971; side #54465F; shadow #2C283B.
Narrow rim highlights #8D7C9E.
Face panel #191621.
Mint eyes #97E4C2, tiny eye highlights #CEF2DF.
Small limb details #568F80 and restrained pink accents #C7849C.
No white body or large bright-white top facet.
Keep the silhouette readable on both dark and light desktop backgrounds.

Revise both side-peeking poses:
Lean the upper body INTO the desktop by approximately 34 degrees.
At the LEFT edge, the upper body leans RIGHT while the lower body stays outside.
At the RIGHT edge, the upper body leans LEFT while the lower body stays outside.
Expose proportionally more of the upper body than the lower body.
Hide the legs and feet completely.
Show both eyes intact and facing the desktop interior.
Redraw a three-quarter face and a bent forearm; one hand grips the screen edge.
The gripping hand stays at a stable contact point rather than rotating away.
Do not use a vertical half-body crop of the neutral standing pose.
Do not rely on a fixed 50 percent exposure rule.

Eight-pose presentation:
Idle, subtle breath, blink, picked-up drag,
left lean-and-peek, right lean-and-peek, bottom rest, top hang.
Keep the planted feet at the same baseline in idle and breath.
Keep the legs attached to the body in the picked-up pose.
Bottom rest remains above the taskbar; top hang preserves the entire face.
Boundary lines and labels are review annotations, not part of the sprite.

Deliver static pose and edge-detail sheets, plus a synthetic desktop scale view.
Include 128 x 128 canvas examples on dark and light backgrounds.
Do not describe these previews as runtime-ready sprites or finished animations.
